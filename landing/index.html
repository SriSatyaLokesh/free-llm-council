The OpenCode Manifesto

Architected for Sovereignty, Accuracy, and Speed

Why a multi-agent council consistently outperforms solitary trillion-parameter models.

Collective Intelligence

Anti-Hallucination

Single models suffer from domain blind spots and confidently stated falsehoods. In a council, multiple independent LLMs examine the query in parallel.

Cross-examination during Stage 2 forces each model to defend its premises, acknowledge convincing counter-arguments, and surface hidden technical tradeoffs.

Economic Sovereignty

$0 / Unlimited

A true multi-agent deliberation requires 25 to 40+ LLM turns per question. On paid cloud APIs, a single debate costs dollars and hits rate limits.

Free LLM Council runs locally through OpenCode (Ollama) and connects to generous free-tier cloud endpoints (Groq, Gemini). Zero subscription fees forever.

Radical Transparency

Blind Scoring

Brand prestige should never dictate truth. During Stage 3, models receive anonymized candidate responses labeled as Response A, B, and C.

Models evaluate arguments strictly on factual rigor, analytical depth, and clarity before a capable Chairman synthesizes the definitive verdict.
